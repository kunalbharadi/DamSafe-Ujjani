The D-Flow official run's native history records initial volume `2,975,439,104 m³`, final `2,615,516,160 m³`, boundary inflow `3,227,098,112 m³`, and outflow `3,587,021,056 m³`; maximum native volume residual is about `7.15e-6 m³`. Map/history volume differences remain within float32 quantization. The lake-at-rest run uses a derived uniform bed and constant water level on the pinned f34 mesh. It is a separate analytical check, not the official case or a site model.

The shared case is a 4 m × 1 m closed, flat channel with a 2 m deep initial column over `x < 1 m`, `2 m³` initial volume per metre of breadth, gravity `9.81 m/s²`, no forcing, and 2 s duration. D-Flow is a horizontal shallow-water model averaged across 1 m breadth; SPH is a vertical x/z section reduced to 0.05 m reporting columns. The common report aligns saved times without extrapolation and compares depths at `x = 0.525, 1.525, 2.525, 3.525 m`: RMSE `0.274, 0.231, 0.304, 0.510 m`. Mean wet-extent IoU is `0.936`; minimum `0.711`. Stage runtimes were `4.265 s` and `142.031 s` under two CPU/2 GiB caps on the same Intel i5-14450HX host. Different discretizations and breaking-wave/impact zones limit interpretation.

The [sensitivity report](evidence/phase2/sensitivity.json) compares each D-Flow variant with the 0.05 m / 0.01 s baseline on common output times: 0.10 m / 0.01 s depth RMSE `0.0769 m`, 0.05 m / 0.005 s `0.0261 m`, and 0.025 m / 0.005 s `0.0394 m`. SPH 0.01 m versus 0.02 m particle spacing differs by `0.160 m` in reported column depth. D-Flow closed-domain volumes remain about `2 m³`; SPH native fluid mass falls from `2,000` to `1,951.2 kg/m` and `1,958.8 kg/m`, respectively. The cause of that loss and the reference coordinates/scaling remain unresolved. A coarser SPH run having lower front-position RMSE does not demonstrate convergence.

One earlier D-Flow shared run exited zero but its native log contained `** ERROR` and its map ended at `0.05 s` instead of `2 s`. The [original false-success manifest](evidence/phase2/dflowfm-shared-c9381c25-a565-418c-958c-7b6c050282b3-initial.json) was retained and the active result corrected to FAILED. The adapter now rejects native solver errors, missing output and truncated duration; a regression test covers native error and truncation. The software suite has 28 passing tests, including absent executable, nonzero exit, cancellation, timeout, corrupt output and stale-worker handling. Software tests are not counted as native smoke runs.

Normalized NetCDF preserves `h`, water-surface elevation, face-centered horizontal velocity components, time, wet/valid masks, coordinates, cell area and provenance. Missing fields remain unavailable. The PartVTK parser rejects corrupt arrays/topology. `prescribed_storage_balance` checks separately documented inflow and release pathways against a monotone level–storage curve and remaining water; it is a diagnostic, **not** a breach-outflow model. No percentage-breach discharge, synthetic Ujjani flood layer, exposure/loss claim, automatic model averaging, or historical-event assessment is supplied.
